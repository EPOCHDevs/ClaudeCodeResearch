Strong DXY moves often precede USD/CAD directional moves.</p> </div> <div> <h4>Confirmation Signals</h4> <p>Wait for DXY to break key levels (91.0, 94.0, 97.0) with volume confirmation before entering USD/CAD positions in the same direction.</p> </div> <div> <h4>Risk Management</h4> <p>Use correlation breakdown as exit signal. If DXY and USD/CAD diverge for more than 48 hours, consider closing position.</p> </div> <div> <p>Pro Tip:</p> <p>DXY correlation is strongest during economic uncertainty.
