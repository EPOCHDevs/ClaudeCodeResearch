# Intra-Day Seasonality in Foreign Exchange Market Transactions

**Authors:** John Cotter, Kevin Dowd
**arXiv:** 1103.5664 (q-fin.TR)
**Submitted:** 29 Mar 2011

## Abstract

This paper examines the intra-day seasonality of transacted limit and market orders in the DEM/USD foreign exchange market. Empirical analysis of completed transactions data based on the Dealing 2000-2 electronic inter-dealer broking system indicates significant evidence of intraday seasonality in returns and return volatilities under usual market conditions. Moreover, analysis of realised tail outcomes supports seasonality for extraordinary market conditions across the trading day.

## Key Points

1. **Data Source:** Dealing 2000-2 electronic inter-dealer broking system
2. **Currency Pair:** DEM/USD (Deutsche Mark / US Dollar)
3. **Order Types Analyzed:** Transacted limit orders and market orders

## Findings

- Significant evidence of intraday seasonality in returns
- Significant evidence of intraday seasonality in return volatilities
- Seasonality present under usual market conditions
- Tail outcomes analysis supports seasonality for extraordinary market conditions
