# Business Cycles and Currency Returns

**Authors:** Riccardo Colacito, Steven J. Riddiough, Lucio Sarno

**Working Paper 26299**
**DOI:** 10.3386/w26299
**Issue Date:** September 2019

## Abstract

We find a strong link between currency excess returns and the relative strength of the business cycle. Buying currencies of strong economies and selling currencies of weak economies generates high returns both in the cross section and time series of countries.

## Key Findings

1. **Returns from Business Cycle Strategy:** Buying currencies of strong economies and selling currencies of weak economies generates high returns

2. **Source of Returns:** These returns stem primarily from spot exchange rate predictability

3. **Uncorrelated with Common Strategies:** Returns are uncorrelated with common currency investment strategies

4. **Not Explained by Traditional Factors:** Cannot be understood using traditional currency risk factors in either unconditional or conditional asset pricing tests

5. **Business Cycle Factor is Priced:** A business cycle factor implied by results is priced in a broad currency cross section

## Implications for FX Seasonality

This research suggests that currency returns are linked to predictable economic cycles, providing a fundamental basis for certain seasonal patterns observed in FX markets.
