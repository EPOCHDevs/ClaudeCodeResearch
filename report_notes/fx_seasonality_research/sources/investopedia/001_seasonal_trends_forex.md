# Seasonal Trends in the Forex Market

Traders in the currency market make one of two choices: pro-dollar or anti-dollar. As a component of more than 85% of all currency transactions, the U.S. dollar has long been the primary driver of fluctuations in exchange rates.

## Key Takeaways

- Traders in any market aim to identify patterns or cycles that they can take advantage of, but these patterns tend to disappear as soon as they are identified.
- In foreign exchange (forex) markets, however, macroeconomic forces can create cycles in currency rates that are sustainable even if they are known.
- Understanding forex seasonality can help forex traders time entries and exits from positions based on patterns that have emerged.
- Such patterns may be driven by powerful economic drivers, but there is no guarantee that they will return.

## Filtering Out Noise

A clear way to analyze past price behavior is to examine the price activity itself without the noise of indicators. When only price is examined, seasonality patterns may emerge.

**Seasonality is a predictable change that repeats every year at the same period of time.** There is no guarantee that a historical pattern will repeat itself, but whenever a pattern has been repeated 80% to 90% of the time, it becomes statistically significant. That is valuable information for a trader.

## October: A Positive Month for USD/JPY

One of the strongest examples of seasonality is the USD/JPY. **In 68% of the samples, USD/JPY ended the month of October higher than where it started.**

It is difficult to pinpoint an exact reason why USD/JPY tends to behave this way in the month of October, but this instance of seasonality is strong.

It's worth keeping in mind for short USD/JPY trades during the month of October. The presence of seasonality may encourage traders to take a smaller-than-usual short position or to avoid a longer-term short USD/JPY trade during this period.

## August: USD/JPY Gains Are Often Erased

There is also a strong case of seasonality in USD/JPY during the month of August. A good portion of the gains earned in July were erased in August. In fact, a look at other yen crosses quickly reveals that **in a calendar year, August tends to be the strongest month for the Japanese yen across the board.**

In other words, other currencies such as the U.S. dollar, euro and British pound have a strong tendency to fall against the yen in August.

## May: A Negative Month for the USD/CAD

For USD/CAD, the strongest case of seasonality is in the months of October and November.

## Implications for Traders

There are many ways that traders can apply the knowledge of seasonality to improve their results:

1. **Long-term traders:** Look for opportunities using fundamentals or technicals to trade in the direction of the seasonal trend.

2. **Short-term traders:** Reduce holding period if taking a trade against the seasonal trend, or focus on trades aligned with seasonality.

## The Bottom Line

Although instances of seasonality in the forex market are rare, being aware of them can help traders become more in tune with the outlook for their currency trades. Seasonal patterns will not always be repeated as the data suggests, but being aware of the trend's stage can help forex traders understand where the probabilities lie.
