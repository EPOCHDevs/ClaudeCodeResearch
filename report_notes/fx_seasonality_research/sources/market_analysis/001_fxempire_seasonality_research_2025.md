# FX Seasonality: What Still Works For Forex Trading in 2025—and What Doesn't

**Source:** FX Empire / Robot Wealth Research

---

## Overview

Comprehensive research article examining which FX seasonality patterns persist in modern markets and which have faded due to market efficiency.

---

## Key Seasonality Patterns Analyzed

### 1. Home vs Away Intraday Effect

**Pattern:** Currencies tend to appreciate during their local trading hours and depreciate during "away" hours.

**Research Findings:**
- Effect has weakened significantly in recent years
- Still observable but much smaller magnitude than historical data suggests
- Possible explanations: increased 24/7 liquidity, algorithmic trading

### 2. Month-End Rebalancing Flows

**Pattern:** Predictable currency flows at month-end due to portfolio rebalancing by institutional investors.

**Research Findings:**
- More robust than intraday patterns
- Driven by real institutional flows
- Timing and magnitude vary by currency pair

### 3. Intraday Seasonality Patterns

**Pattern:** Time-of-day effects in currency returns and volatility.

**Analysis includes:**
- London open effects
- New York open effects
- Session overlap patterns
- Volatility clustering by hour

## Key Conclusions

1. **Persistence varies:** Some patterns remain tradeable, others have faded
2. **Market efficiency:** Crowding and algorithmic trading have eroded some effects
3. **Need for adaptation:** Strategies require continuous monitoring and adjustment
4. **Combination approach:** Multiple seasonality factors may work better than single patterns
