<div><div>Citation Excerpt :</div><p>Since there is currently no consensus regarding the best measure of uncertainty, many distinct proxies have been employed in the prior literature. According to Fernandes et al. (2014) and Mele et al. (2015), the Chicago Board Options Exchanges (CBOE) implied volatility index (VIX) is the most commonly used proxy for global financial market uncertainty.1 However, the VIX may not be linked to true economic uncertainty, since it might be driven by factors associated with time-varying risk-aversion rather than economic uncertainty (Bekaert et al., 2013).</p></div>
