# Beyond Fear: Can the VIX Offer Operational Insights or Predict Volatility?

[Domenico D'Errico]<web_link>

2 min read

Apr 15, 2025

--

Listen

Share

Apart from signaling market fear, can the VIX actually provide operational insights? Can it reliably predict future volatility?

To explore this, let’s quickly calculate the weekly average of the VIX index and compare it to the average realized volatility of the S&P 500, shifted forward by four weeks.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

When we examine the chart showing the VIX tracking error — essentially, how accurately it predicts the S&P 500’s 30-day realized volatility — it becomes evident that most of its forecasts miss the mark. During periods of market turmoil, the VIX tends to significantly overestimate future volatility. In practical terms, relying on the VIX for guidance during financial panic is a bit like asking someone escaping a burning building where the loom is — not particularly helpful for you, and definitely not for them.

While the VIX is a useful sentiment gauge, it’s not a particularly accurate forecasting tool. If we want to develop robust volatility models or risk management strategies, we clearly need something different.
