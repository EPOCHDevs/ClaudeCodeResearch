AI Definitions

The MM Business Cycle Equity Debt Allocation Score is based on the macroeconomics data for each economic cycle, such as consumption, employment, inflation, and relevant indicators of bank credit demand and supply, and also takes into account monetary policy tightness, business cycles, and credit expansion/contraction levels, etc. The score dynamically reflects the asset allocation of stocks and bonds (S&P 500 and US government bonds) that maintains a long-term and relatively stable investment portfolio. Equity-debt allocation ratio: Score greater than 80 points, stock: bond → 7: 3 Score greater than 60 points, stock: bond → 6 : 4 Score greater than 50 points, stock: bond → 5 : 5 Score greater than 40 points, stock: bond → 4 : 6 Score greater than 30 points, stock: bond → 3:7 Score less than 30 points, stock: bond → 2:8 Dynamically adjusting the weight of stock and bond allocation through an economic cycle can reduce the volatility of an investment portfolio and smooth the equity curve of its long-term investment when the stock market undergoes a major correction.
