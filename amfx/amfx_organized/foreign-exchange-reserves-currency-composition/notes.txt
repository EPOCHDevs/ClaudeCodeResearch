Title: Foreign Exchange Reserves Composition by Currency (2000-2022)
Source: AMFX 30MAR23.pdf (page 2)
Date: 2023-03-30
Type: chart

Insight: Stacked area chart showing the declining share of U.S. dollar reserves in global foreign exchange holdings from 2000 to 2022, with notable reduction during 2003-2007 as alternative currencies gained share.

Context (above):
If anything, the dollar is most stable while the EUR and JPY are losing popularity around the world. It is true that central 
banks reduced their USD reserves from 2000 to 2010. The chart looks like this:

Context (below):
There was a meaningful reduction in USD reserves as a percentage of the overall stack in the 2000s. This was mostly 
centered on the 2003-2007 period as the external value of the dollar was falling and there was an off-and-on panic 
about the USD as a store of value in those days. Here is the external value of the USD since 1972.
