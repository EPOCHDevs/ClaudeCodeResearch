Insight: This NASDAQ price chart marks Monday of quarterly expiration weeks to illustrate a cyclical pattern in equity momentum that has repeated seven quarters in a row, suggesting a potential trading signal based on reversing the prior 10-day momentum direction.

Context (above):
NASDAQ with Monday of quarterly expiry week marked

Context (below):
The next chart shows the P&L of looking at (do the opposite of whatever happened in the 10 days leading into the 
Monday before quarterly OPEX and cover 10 days after OPEX). So right now, stocks are up over the past 10 days so 
this silly model would say short NQ and cover 10 trading days after this Friday. Again, I know this is kind of random, but 
the rhythmic, cyclical turning of equity momentum at quarterly options expiry has worked seven quarters in a row back 
to September 2021 so it’s at least interesting if not statistically significant.
