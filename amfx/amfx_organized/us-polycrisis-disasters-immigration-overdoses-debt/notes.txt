We live in the age of polycrisis. A seemingly endless list of grave challenges has emerged in this new millennium with 
deaths of despair, political polarization, financial instability, economic bifurcation, global war, an obesity epidemic, rising 
p(doom) from AI, climate change, and more. Sure, some if this is just “history happening” as Niall Ferguson puts it, but 
anyone who has lived both the 1990s and the 2020s can plainly see all is not well. While the 1990s were particularly 
harmonious and happy, the 2020s are particularly dissonant and dark.
