Title: Mental wellbeing in young adulthood (ages 18-24) improves with older age of first smartphone
Source: AMFX 09FEB24.pdf (page 7)
Date: 2024-02-09
Type: chart

Insight: Young adults who received their first smartphone at older ages show significantly better mental health outcomes (higher MHQ scores and lower distress rates) compared to those who obtained smartphones at younger ages.
