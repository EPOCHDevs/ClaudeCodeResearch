Title: Historical Yield Curve Inversions and Recession Timing
Source: AMFX   Recession For and Against  23NOV22 (1).pdf (page 3)
Date: 2022-11-23
Type: table

Insight: This table tracks the relationship between 3-month to 10-year yield curve inversions and subsequent recessions, showing an average lag of 311 calendar days between inversion and recession onset.

Context (above):
There are some compelling arguments for US recession in 2023. First and foremost are the aggressive and deepening 
yield curve inversions. Jim Bianco had a good thread on inversions last night; this table stood out to me.

Context (below):
311 days from now is September 30, 2023. The range of lags has been 140 to 487 days which puts the range wide but 
real: US recession starts sometime between April 12, 2023, and March 24, 2024. Don’t rule out 2023 Fed cuts.
