Source: AMFX 20AUG24.pdf (page 6)

Insight: Historical chart showing CFTC speculative Japanese Yen futures positioning from 1992 to 2024, with the current position highlighted in red, indicating a significant shift in trader sentiment that typically precedes directional price movement.

Context (above):
On balance, the price action has tended to follow the direction of the big positioning 
shift. That bodes ill for the likes of NZD/JPY, as the kiwi futures position has dropped 
by just over 5 sigma over the last six weeks as well. Granted, the sample size of the 
study above is not particularly large, but the only time when the price action clearly 
moved against the positioning was a Russell 2000 rally nearly 20 years ago.
