Title: Well-Being and Life Satisfaction by Household Income
Source: AMFX 01APR22.pdf (page 2)
Date: 2022-04-01
Type: chart

Insight: Both experienced well-being and life satisfaction increase with household income, with a notable acceleration in life satisfaction gains after the $75,000 income threshold.

Context (below):
Mean levels of experienced well-being (real-time feeling reports on a good–bad continuum) 
and evaluative well-being (overall life satisfaction) for each income band. Income axis is log transformed.
