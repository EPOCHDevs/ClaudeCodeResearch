Title: Distribution of daily EURUSD closes >1.08 since Aug2022
Source: AMFX 20AUG24.pdf (page 2)
Date: 2024-08-20
Type: chart

Insight: This histogram shows that EURUSD closes above 1.08 have been heavily concentrated in the 1.0800-1.1050 range since August 2022, with very few occurrences above 1.1100, highlighting the rarity of the current elevated price levels.

Context (above):
Much like USDCAD, the euro is right at a huge level as we have closed above 1.1100 just nine times in the past 
two years. We have only closed above 1.1130 five times in the last two years. We are in rarified air.
