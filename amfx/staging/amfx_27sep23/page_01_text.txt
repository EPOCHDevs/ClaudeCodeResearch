Stocks are following the seasonal pattern incredibly well through the Fall Equinox and 
the focus now is on the JPM collar (4210), and a bunch of levels down in the 4200/4250 
area. I still think it’s a bit early to be getting long for bullish yearend seasonality, but if 
the Price is Right®, it’s not crazy to go a bit early. The seasonality is well-known and 
that means markets will want to get ahead of it, especially as plenty of traders seem to 
have caught the down move. Recency bias ensures that if one seasonality trade works, 
the next trade will be spotlighted and oversubscribed as speculators front run it. 
 
I published the year-end seasonality around pre-election years yesterday, but the year-
end effect is not highly conditional. For example, 2011 and 2015 were fierce bear 
market years, and the effect still worked. 1995 and 1999 were fierce bull markets, and 
the effect worked. Here are the average seasonal paths of the SPX, NASDAQ, Russell
