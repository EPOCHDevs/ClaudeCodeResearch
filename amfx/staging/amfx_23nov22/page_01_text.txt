Third, something else to keep an eye on is the path of US yields from here. After CPI, 
we gapped down from 4.07% to 3.90% and have been etching out a clean range now 
3.67/3.90. Yields have been respecting the top of the gap and we have now spent 11 
days consolidating. My view is that the next move in yields is lower and if that view is 
correct, we should not go back above 4.0%.
