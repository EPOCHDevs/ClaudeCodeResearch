Title: 2025 Was The Year Of Increased Capital Expenditures
Topic: This chart compares Magnificent 7 (ex-Tesla) capital expenditures totals in the first three quarters of 2024 versus the first three quarters of 2025, measured in billions of dollars.
Question: How did capital spending by mega-cap tech companies change between 2024 and 2025?
Key Takeaways:
- Capital spending increased broadly in 2025 across the Magnificent 7 (excluding Tesla), with expenditures through the first three calendar quarters of 2025 higher than the same period in 2024 for every company shown.
- Amazon, Google, Microsoft, and Meta accounted for the majority of spending increases, with Amazon leading at roughly $85 billion and Google at approximately $38 billion in 2024 growing further in 2025.
- The chart highlights that the largest companies in the market prioritized long-term investment in 2025, reflecting a focus on infrastructure and growth initiatives such as AI.
