Title: A Manual for Bull Markets
Topic: This chart tracks S&P 500 returns during previous bull markets since 1949 (defined as a 20% or greater rise following a 20% or more decline), along with the historical average return of 192%.
Question: How do S&P 500 returns during historical bull markets compare to each other and to the current bull market?
Key Takeaways:
- On average, bull markets have delivered substantial returns of 192%, with some standout periods such as 1982-1987 (229%), 1987-2000 (582%), and 2009-2020 (401%).
- The current bull market since October 2022 has gained approximately 92%, which remains below the historical average of 192% for previous bull markets, suggesting potential room for further growth.
- Historical data from bull markets highlights the potential rewards of staying invested throughout market cycles, as significant gains are often realized by those who remain in the market during these extended periods of growth.
