Title: Retail Sales
Topic: This chart presents the month-over-month percentage change in retail sales along with a 3-month moving average for the past year, reflecting consumer spending patterns and economic health.
Question: How have monthly retail sales been trending, and what does the recent data suggest about consumer spending strength?
Key Takeaways:
- The monthly change in retail sales in December 2025 was -0.02%, and over the past 3 months the monthly change has averaged 0.13%.
- Retail sales showed notable variability throughout 2025, with a peak of 1.82% growth in April 2025 and a trough of -0.38% in October 2025.
- Consistent growth in retail sales can suggest a strong economy beneficial for businesses, corporate earnings, and the stock market, while prolonged weakness may signal consumer hesitation or economic uncertainty.
