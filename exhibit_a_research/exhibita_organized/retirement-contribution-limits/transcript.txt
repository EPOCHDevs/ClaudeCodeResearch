Title: Employee 401(k) Contribution Limit For 2026 Is $24.5K
Topic: This chart shows the annual employee 401(k) contribution limit from 2006 through 2026, tracking how the IRS has gradually increased the maximum amount individuals can contribute to their employer-sponsored retirement plans on a pre-tax or Roth basis.
Question: How have 401(k) employee contribution limits changed over time, and what is the current limit for 2026?
Key Takeaways:
- The employee 401(k) contribution limit for 2026 is $24,500, up from $15,000 in 2006, representing a cumulative increase of $9,500 (63%) over the 20-year period.
- The limit has increased in a stair-step pattern, with periodic $500 increases (and occasional flat years), reflecting IRS adjustments for inflation; notable jumps occurred from $15,000 to $15,500 in 2007 and the limit held steady at certain levels for multiple years (e.g., $18,000 from 2015-2017).
- Reviewing these limits annually can help individuals stay informed about available tax-advantaged saving opportunities, and evaluating contribution levels in the context of personal financial goals and overall circumstances is an important part of retirement planning.
