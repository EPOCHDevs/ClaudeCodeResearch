Title: Most Years See Big Gains or Big Losses
Topic: This chart compares the average up year versus the average down year in the S&P 500 since 1950, highlighting the substantial volatility in annual returns from year to year.
Question: How do average gains in up years compare to average losses in down years for the S&P 500?
Key Takeaways:
- The average "up" year in the S&P 500 since 1950 has returned +17.7%, while the average "down" year has seen a decline of -11.8%.
- Years with significant positive returns have been counterbalanced by years with large negative returns, showing that big swings in either direction are normal market behavior.
- Long-term investment success has historically been dependent on remaining disciplined and staying invested through both the large gains and the substantial losses.
