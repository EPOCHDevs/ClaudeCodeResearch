Title: Age Shapes Our Perception of Risk
Topic: This chart shows how many S&P 500 bear markets (defined as a drop of 20% or more from a previous high) a person would have lived through based on their current age, illustrating how life experience shapes risk perception.
Question: How many bear markets has someone of a given age experienced in their lifetime, and how does that shape their view of market risk?
Key Takeaways:
- The number of S&P 500 bear markets experienced varies significantly by age: A 25-year-old has lived through 4 bear markets, while a 90-year-old has experienced 17, creating vastly different frames of reference for market volatility.
- Age shapes our perception of risk: It is natural for different age groups to react differently to market swings because the more history you have lived through, the more your understanding of risk and recovery evolves.
- Someone age 30 has lived through 4 bear markets while someone age 70 has lived through 11: Age and experience shape how each person views risk and responds to market volatility, which is critical for advisors to understand when working with clients.
