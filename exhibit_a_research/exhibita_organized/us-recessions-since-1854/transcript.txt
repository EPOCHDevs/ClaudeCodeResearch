Title: 35 Recessions in the U.S. Since 1854
Topic: This chart visualizes all 35 U.S. recessions since 1854 as defined by the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER), with shaded periods indicating recession. It shows that recessions have become less frequent and shorter in duration in more recent decades compared to earlier periods.
Question: How frequently do recessions occur in the U.S., and have they changed over time?
Key Takeaways:
- Since 1854, the U.S. economy has experienced 35 recessions, making economic contractions a recurring and unavoidable aspect of the economic cycle.
- Recessions were notably more frequent and longer in duration in earlier periods (1854-1940s) compared to more recent decades, where they have become less frequent.
- The unpredictable nature of recessions suggests that trying to time the market based on recession predictions is a challenging and often ineffective investment strategy.
