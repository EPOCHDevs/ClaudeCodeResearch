Question: How frequently do 5% pullbacks occur within a given year in the S&P 500?
Key Takeaways:
- 5% pullbacks are a normal part of market behavior; in strong years the market can still experience multiple intra-year declines of 5% or more due to sentiment shifts, economic events, or other factors.
- The frequency has varied from 0 to 5 pullbacks per year since 1990, with most years experiencing at least one such pullback.
