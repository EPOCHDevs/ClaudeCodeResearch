Title: Major Country Equity Performance in 2025
Topic: This chart shows the full-year 2025 total returns of major country equity markets including Brazil, Canada, Germany, United Kingdom, China, France, Japan, United States, Australia, and India, arranged from highest to lowest.
Question: How did major country equity markets perform in 2025, and which countries delivered the strongest and weakest returns?
Key Takeaways:
- Brazil was the top-performing country equity market in 2025 with a total return of 48.5%, followed by Canada at 35.8%, Germany at 35.7%, and the United Kingdom at 34.6%.
- The United States delivered a total return of 17.9%, ranking 8th out of the 10 countries shown, while India was the weakest performer at just 2.7%, and Australia returned 13.3%.
- International markets broadly outperformed the U.S. in 2025, underscoring the importance of geographic diversification and the cyclical nature of country-level equity performance driven by global and local economic conditions.
