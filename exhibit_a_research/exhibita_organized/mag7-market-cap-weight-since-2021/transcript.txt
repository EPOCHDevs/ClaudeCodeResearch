Title: Mag 7 Weight in the S&P 500
Topic: This chart tracks the combined market cap weight of the Magnificent 7 stocks (Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Nvidia, and Tesla) in the S&P 500 since 2021, showing their growing concentration.
Question: How has the Magnificent 7's share of the S&P 500 by market capitalization changed since 2021?
Key Takeaways:
- The Mag 7 weight in the S&P 500 reached 33.7% as of February 27, 2026, meaning just seven companies account for more than a third of the entire index by market capitalization.
- The combined influence has steadily risen from roughly 20-22% in early 2021 to over 33% by early 2026, reflecting both strong performance and growing dominance in the index.
- This trend illustrates how the S&P 500 has become more concentrated in recent years and serves as a reminder of the importance of diversification beyond the largest names.
