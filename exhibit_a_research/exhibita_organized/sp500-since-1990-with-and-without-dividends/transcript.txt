Title: The Importance of Dividends
Topic: This chart compares the growth of $1 invested in the S&P 500 since 1990 with dividends reinvested versus without dividends reinvested. It demonstrates the powerful compounding effect that reinvested dividends have on long-term investment growth.
Question: How much of an impact do reinvested dividends have on long-term S&P 500 investment returns?
Key Takeaways:
- As of February 27, 2026, a $1 investment in the S&P 500 with dividends reinvested (since 1990) would have grown to $39.78, while the same investment without dividends reinvested would have grown to only $19.17.
- Reinvesting dividends roughly doubled the total return over this period, highlighting the compounding effect of dividends as a critical driver of long-term wealth creation.
- The chart emphasizes the value of a buy-and-hold investment strategy that includes reinvesting dividends, as the compounding effect dramatically enhances growth over time.
