Title: Historical Bull and Bear Markets in the S&P 500
Topic: This chart displays the percentage gain or loss in all S&P 500 bull and bear markets since 1942. It contrasts the magnitude and duration of bull markets (rising periods) against bear markets (declines of 20% or more) to show the asymmetry between gains and losses.
Question: How do historical bull market gains compare to bear market losses in the S&P 500?
Key Takeaways:
- Bull markets have historically produced far larger gains than the losses experienced during bear markets, with some bull runs exceeding 400-600% gains, while bear markets typically involve declines of 20-50%.
- The current bull market, which began on 10/12/2022, has produced a 92% gain as of the chart date, demonstrating strong ongoing recovery.
- While bear markets can feel uncomfortable, they have historically been followed by substantial bull markets, reinforcing the importance of staying invested through downturns rather than attempting to time the market.
