Title: What if you panic sold and missed April 9th?
Topic: This chart compares the S&P 500 year-to-date path for 2025 when remaining fully invested versus missing the best day of the year (April 9th), showing how one single missed day can dramatically alter returns.
Question: What is the cost of panic selling and missing the best single trading day in a given year?
Key Takeaways:
- Missing one day can wreck a year: The market's strongest days often arrive after periods of high fear and volatility, and emotional short-term decisions like panic selling can cause lasting damage to long-term performance.
- Through February 27, 2026, the S&P 500 has gained 18.7% year-to-date, but when you remove April 9th (the best day of 2025), returns fall off a cliff, dropping to 8.4%, showing how one missed day can erase a meaningful chunk of progress.
- This chart illustrates how dangerous it is to try and time the market: Missing just one large up day in any given year can have a large impact on returns, reinforcing the importance of staying invested through volatility.
