Topic: This chart overlays the S&P 500 price since 1990 with dots marking the 25 best and 25 worst daily performances, showing that extreme market days tend to cluster around periods of heightened volatility rather than being evenly distributed.
Question: Do the best and worst days in the stock market occur randomly, or do they tend to cluster together?
Key Takeaways:
- The 25 best and 25 worst daily performances in the S&P 500 tend to cluster around periods of heightened market volatility, meaning extreme up and down days often occur in close proximity to each other.
- Despite major swings in both directions, the long-term trajectory of the S&P 500 remains upward, with the index reaching approximately 6,878 as shown on the chart.
- Many of the best days happen shortly after the worst ones, emphasizing how stepping out of the market during turbulent periods risks missing key recovery gains that are critical to long-term returns.
