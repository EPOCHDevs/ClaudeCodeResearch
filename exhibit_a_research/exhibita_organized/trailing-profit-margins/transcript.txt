Title: A Look at Trailing Profit Margins
Topic: Trailing profit margins based on the past 12 months of net income and revenue for the S&P 500 (Large Cap), S&P 400 (Mid Cap), and S&P 600 (Small Cap) since 2004. This chart tracks the efficiency and profitability of businesses across different market capitalization segments over time.
Question: How have trailing profit margins for large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap U.S. companies trended over the past 20 years, and where do they stand today?
Key Takeaways:
- As of February 27, 2026, trailing profit margins stand at 13.80% for the S&P 500 (Large Cap), 7.52% for the S&P 400 (Mid Cap), and 5.69% for the S&P 600 (Small Cap).
- Over the past 20 years, trailing profit margins have generally trended upward for large-cap companies, while mid-cap and small-cap margins have remained more range-bound, with large caps consistently showing higher margins.
- Profit margin trends provide insight into overall corporate efficiency and profitability, and the widening gap between large-cap and smaller-cap margins suggests large companies have increasingly benefited from scale advantages and operational efficiencies.
