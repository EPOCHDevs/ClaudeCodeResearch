Title: Feelings About The U.S. Economy
Topic: This chart tracks the University of Michigan Consumer Sentiment Index since 1978, a widely followed measure of U.S. consumer confidence reflecting how optimistic or pessimistic consumers feel about the economy, job prospects, and personal finances.
Question: How do American consumers currently feel about the economy, and how does present sentiment compare to historical levels?
Key Takeaways:
- As of January 2026, the Consumer Sentiment Index stood at 56.4, which is well below the long-term historical average and suggests subdued consumer confidence.
- Consumer sentiment plays a critical role in economic growth as it influences consumer spending, one of the largest drivers of the U.S. economy.
- The index has experienced significant swings over the decades, with notable lows during recessions (early 1980s, 2008 financial crisis, and 2022), and the current reading remains in a historically depressed range.
