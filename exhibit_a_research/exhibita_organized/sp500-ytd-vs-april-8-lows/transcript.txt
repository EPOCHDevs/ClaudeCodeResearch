Title: Staying The Course Worked In 2025
Topic: This chart compares year-to-date returns across major equity categories for two scenarios: investors who held throughout 2025 versus those who sold during the April low panic.
Question: What was the cost of selling at the April 2025 lows versus staying invested across different equity categories?
Key Takeaways:
- Investors who stayed the course were rewarded across all equity categories in 2025, with Emerging Markets returning approximately 30.8%, Developed Markets about 27.0%, U.S. Large Cap around 18.4%, U.S. Mid Cap roughly 5.9%, and U.S. Small Cap about 4.3%.
- Those who sold at the April lows locked in significant negative returns across every category, with losses ranging from approximately -10% to -30%.
- The data underscores the stark difference in outcomes for those who panicked and sold versus those who stayed invested, reinforcing the importance of discipline during market drawdowns.
