# AMFX 29MAY24 - The South Park Jinx

**Date:** May 29, 2024
**Author:** Brent Donnelly, Spectra Markets
**Pages:** 6 (including disclaimer)

## Summary

The newsletter investigates whether South Park appearing to feature a publicly-traded company serves as a contrarian indicator (similar to the Magazine Cover Indicator or the Madden Jinx). The author compiles a comprehensive table of 30+ South Park episodes that featured publicly-traded companies and measures their subsequent stock performance relative to the S&P 500. The median stock underperforms the S&P 500 by 7% one year after appearing on South Park. The piece also discusses the "Windowmaker No More" thesis on JGB yields diverging from US yields, and notes the launch of the Roundhill GLP-1 ETF (OZEM) as a potential reverse indicator for GLP-1 stocks.

### Table 1: South Park Jinx -- Company Performance After Episode Airing

- **type:** Data table with color-coded performance cells
- **image_file:** page_02_img_00.png
- **title:** South Park episodes, companies, and ticker symbols with Performance minus SPX
- **page:** 2
- **data:** Complete list of South Park episodes featuring publicly-traded companies, with stock ticker and relative performance vs. S&P 500 at 3-month, 6-month, and 1-year horizons after the episode air date
- **assets/instruments:** NVO US, DIS US, MSFT US, NFLX US, PFE US, WBA US, MMEN US, CMCSA US, AMZN US, TLRY US, MNK US, META US, TWTR US, YELP US, MSFT US, SONY US, TWX US, QRTEA US, EA US, FDX US, YUM US, MAT US, WMT US, TEVA US, NVS US, PFE US, SBUX US (30+ stocks total)
- **transforms/calculations:** Relative performance = stock total return minus S&P 500 total return over same period; average, median, and win rate calculated
- **metrics:**
  - **Average performance minus SPX:** 3m: -4%, 6m: -6%, 1y: -6%
  - **Median performance minus SPX:** 3m: 0%, 6m: -7%, 1y: -7%
  - **Win rate (stock beats SPX):** 3m: 52%, 6m: 41%, 1y: 39%
- **time_range:** Episode dates from December 16, 1998 to May 24, 2024
- **x_axis:** N/A (table format)
- **y_axis:** N/A (table format)
- **insight:** South Park is a contrarian indicator. One year after appearing on South Park, the median stock underperforms the S&P 500 by 7%, and the win rate drops to 39%. The logic is that if a company is prominent enough to be featured on South Park, it has already had massive outperformance and is at peak cultural zeitgeist -- ripe for mean reversion. Notable outliers: NFLX +101.2% at 1y (streaming wars episode 2022), MMEN -99.8% at 1y (MedMen, cannabis).
- **source:** Brent Donnelly research, various websites, Google, AI
