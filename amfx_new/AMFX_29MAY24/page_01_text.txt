The South Park Jinx 
 
Citrini Research, an excellent follow on 
Twitter, posted the tweet at right the 
other day. Sol pointed me to it. The tweet 
is funny because it's true. South Park has 
a 20+-year history of capturing the 
cultural zeitgeist and it’s impossible to 
argue that anything that is lampooned 
on South Park is not priced in. 
 
That got me thinking… Is there a possible 
market signal when a company is 
featured on the show? The logic would 
be similar to the Magazine Cover 
Indicator, or The Madden Jinx. By 
definition, anything featured is well-
known and at risk of mean reversion 
after massive outperformance.
