# AMFX 21DEC22 - Analysis

## PDF Title and Date
**Title:** Optimism feels crazy right now
**Date:** December 21, 2022
**Author:** Brent Donnelly, Spectra Markets

## Summary
The newsletter presents five contrarian bullish arguments for equities heading into 2023, despite the overwhelming bearish consensus (recession, earnings downgrades, bonds as alternative to stocks). The five reasons: (1) basic stats lean bullish after down years (average SPX return 9.9% after a down year vs. 6.3% after an up year), (2) falling gas prices provide consumer stimulus, (3) bubble froth has been removed (AMZN and ARKK round trips), (4) earnings pessimism may be overdone (Nike/FedEx positive surprises), (5) soft data may be wrong in a nominal economy. The sidebar discusses Japan capital flows and USDJPY. The attached positioning report covers G10 FX scores and 2023 consensus FX strategy forecasts with a historical analog to the 1994/1995 rate cycle.
