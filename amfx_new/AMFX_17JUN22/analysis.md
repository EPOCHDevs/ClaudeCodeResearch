# AMFX 17JUN22 - Analysis

## PDF Title and Date
**Title:** EUR BTD
**Date:** Friday, June 17, 2022
**Author:** Brent Donnelly, Spectra Markets

## Summary
The newsletter discusses the ECB pledge to stabilize peripheral spreads and its impact on EURUSD. Donnelly argues the EURUSD selloff is a buying opportunity, supported by tightening Germany/Italy spreads, rate differentials, and European equity outperformance vs. US equities. He goes long EURUSD at 1.0488. The "fun fact" section covers etymology of "argue" and a YouGov survey on couples' arguments.
