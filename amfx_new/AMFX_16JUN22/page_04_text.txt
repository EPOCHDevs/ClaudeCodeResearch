Flashback 
I still remember the Bear Stearns liquidations by JPM in March 2008. Those were a fierce example of how markets 
can go completely nuts and dislocate completely from correlated variables, oversold signals and so on when forced 
liquidation happens. The execution person doesn’t really care about what bid they hit. The risk manager behind them 
is yelling: “Just hit a bid!” 
 
In 2008, I was trading EURJPY at Lehman Brothers, and the currency pair was just about 100% correlated to high-
yield credit and the S&P 500. The market was in complete disarray. Volatility was at all-time highs. EURJPY was a 
risky asset proxy so if SPX and HYG went up, EURJPY would go up… 99 times out of 100 (and vice versa). I actively 
traded these correlations and I still have vivid memories of how boggled my mind was as HYG ripped and EURJPY 
would just keep getting pummeled by JPM in wave after wave of selling.
