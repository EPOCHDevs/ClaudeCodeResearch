### Chart 1: EURGBP vs. Germany/UK 10-Year Rate Differential

- **type:** Dual-axis overlay line chart
- **image_file:** page_01_img_02.png (top half)
- **title:** EURGBP vs. Germany/UK 10-year rate differential
- **page:** 1
- **data:** EURGBP spot rate overlaid with the spread between German and UK 10-year government bond yields
- **assets/instruments:** EURGBP, German 10-year Bund yield, UK 10-year Gilt yield
- **transforms/calculations:** 10-year rate differential (Germany minus UK), overlay comparison
- **metrics:** Rate differential in percentage points, EURGBP exchange rate levels (~0.85-0.90 range visible)
- **time_range:** Approximately March 2023 to June 2023 (roughly 3 months of hourly/daily data)
- **x_axis:** Date (Mar through Jun 2023)
- **y_axis:** Left axis: rate differential; Right axis: EURGBP spot rate (~0.85-0.90)
- **insight:** The divergence between EURGBP and rate differentials that existed after UK CPI has now closed. Rate differentials initially decoupled from EURGBP as the "sell gilts, sell GBP" trade dominated, but over time, juicier UK nominal yields attracted money into GBP, closing the gap. The currency is no longer dislocated.
- **source:** TradingView (visible watermark)
