# AMFX 20AUG24 - Analysis

## PDF Title and Date
**Title:** Big Levels
**Date:** August 20, 2024
**Author:** Brent Donnelly, Spectra Markets

## Summary
The newsletter covers two major FX themes: (1) USDCAD approaching the critical 1.3600 support level with two idiosyncratic CAD-bearish factors (Couche-Tard/7-Eleven M&A and Canadian rail worker strike risks), and (2) EURUSD trading at extreme highs near 1.1100/1.1130, described as "rarified air" with only 9 daily closes above 1.1100 in the past two years. The second half features the Spectra FX Weekly Positioning Report (guest-written excerpt from Cameron Crise / Macro Man) analyzing extreme futures positioning shifts, particularly the JPY flip, plus full G10 FX positioning and momentum scorecards.
