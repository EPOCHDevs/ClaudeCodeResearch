The Spectra FX Positioning and Momentum Report 
Flipping Yen 
 
 
Hi. Welcome to this week’s report. There has been a complete 180 in many metrics over the past few weeks as 
the CFTC has exited mega short JPY positions and now flipped long. USD momentum has been extremely bearish 
of late as we slow-motion crash up towards the top of the EURUSD range. Key levels in EURUSD are heavy in the 
1.1100/1.1130 zone. 
 
In lieu of the normal write up this week, I am excerpting from Cameron Crise’s excellent piece this week because 
it captures the yen flip nicely and makes some interesting observations about future performance of various 
securities after a multi-sigma possy flip. The following excerpt is provided with Cam’s permission.
