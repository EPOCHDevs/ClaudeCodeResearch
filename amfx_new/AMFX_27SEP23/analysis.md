# AMFX 27SEP23 - Is normalization good or bad?

**Date:** September 27, 2023
**Author:** Brent Donnelly, Spectra Markets
**Pages:** 4 (including disclaimer)

## Summary

The newsletter discusses two main topics: (1) year-end equity seasonality in pre-election years, presenting average seasonal paths for four major indices suggesting a bullish entry window around early October, and (2) whether interest rate normalization (return to positive real rates) is good or bad for the economy and risk assets. The author argues both sides have merit but expresses concern about the MOF (Ministry of Finance, Japan) intervention strategies for USDJPY near 150. The tone is cautious-bullish on equities into year-end, with a focus on the JPM collar at 4200/4250 as a key level.

- **image_file:** page_01_img_02.png
- **title:** Seasonal path of four major equity indices, 1998 to 2023
- **page:** 1

- **assets/instruments:** S&P 500 (SPX), NASDAQ, Russell 2000, DAX

- **metrics:**
  - S&P 500: seasonal trough ~09OCT, range roughly flat to +8%
  - NASDAQ: seasonal trough ~02OCT/08OCT, range roughly flat to +14%
  - Russell 2000: seasonal trough ~09OCT, range roughly flat to +8%
  - DAX: seasonal trough ~02OCT, range roughly flat to +8%
- **time_range:** Calendar year (Jan-Dec), computed across pre-election years 1998-2023
- **x_axis:** Months within a calendar year (Jan through Dec)
- **y_axis:** Average cumulative return (percentage, varies by panel)
- **insight:** All four indices show a consistent seasonal pattern of bottoming in early-to-mid October and rallying into year-end during pre-election years. The sweet spot for entry is 01OCT/09OCT. The year-end effect is not highly conditional -- it worked in both bear (2011, 2015) and bull (1995, 1999) market years. Annotated entry points are marked on each panel.
