# AMFX 02AUG23 - Analysis

## PDF Metadata
- **Title:** Unhinged
- **Date:** August 2, 2023
- **Author:** Brent Donnelly, Spectra Markets
- **Pages:** 5
- **Theme:** US fiscal policy is "completely unhinged" -- the Fitch downgrade is a reminder that procyclical MMT-style spending has broken the historical unemployment/deficit relationship. Also covers the oil market (API crude inventory draw, RBOB gasoline bull flag) and ISM Manufacturing data.

## Summary
Donnelly argues the Fitch downgrade is irrelevant as market news but highlights the structural shift in US fiscal policy. He shows that the historical negative relationship between unemployment and budget deficits has broken down since ~2017 (Trump TCJA), with the 2020s running massive deficits despite low unemployment. He then pivots to oil markets -- API crude inventories had their largest draw in 40 years, crude oil is approaching a potential breakout above the $83.50 equilibrium zone, and RBOB gasoline is forming a bull flag. Finally, he notes ISM Manufacturing vs. New Orders are stabilizing. Current views: Flat (no positions).
