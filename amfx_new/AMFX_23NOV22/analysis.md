# AMFX 23NOV22 - Recession: For and Against

**Date:** November 23, 2022
**Author:** Brent Donnelly, Spectra Markets
**Pages:** 7

## Summary

This newsletter provides a balanced "for and against" analysis of the probability of a US recession in 2023. The bullish-recession case includes yield curve inversion history, LEI deterioration, weak soft data (NFIB), and rising credit card debt. The counter-argument highlights strong labor markets (JOLTS still at 10+ million openings), the buffer from the massive job shortage, easing financial conditions, and the unique nature of this cycle. Donnelly concludes it is early to position for recession; the best recession trade would be short cross/JPY. Also covers S&P 500 Thanksgiving seasonality, US 10-year yield technical analysis, and art history (Holbein's "The Ambassadors").

**Current Views mentioned:** Long TYZ2 @ 112-17 (stop 111-15), Short AUDUSD @ 0.6711 (stop 0.6811, TP 0.6555).
