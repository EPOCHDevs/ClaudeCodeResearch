Insight: This multi-panel chart shows AUDNZD spot price versus fair value implied by macro factors, percentage deviation, and z-score signals, indicating the pair is elevated above fundamental fair value of ~1.13 and presents a selling opportunity.

Context (above):
Is the USD overbought? In short: no. Our positioning indicator, GPS (Global Positioning Score), shows the market  
has only recently flipped from short to long USD. The first chart plots our broad USD measure, aggregating G10 and EM  
positioning. It highlights the sharp swing around Liberation Day—from extreme longs to extreme shorts in a matter of  
months—but since bottoming last summer, USD shorts have been gradually reduced, not abruptly reversed. At current  
levels, USD positioning does not look stretched, and we don’t see extreme positioning as a reason to fade the move.  
The composition of GPS shows USD longs are more pronounced versus EM than G10. Under the hood, MXN shorts look  
somewhat extended, which argues for patience. We prefer waiting for better levels to sell CADMXN, with our  
macro-factor framework placing fair value around 13.30. Elsewhere, AUD and CLP remain vulnerable to a further  
unwind of long positioning. While risk-reversal signals in both have shifted toward USD strength, CTA and macro hedge  
fund components remain long, suggesting positioning has further room to normalize. We also continue to like selling  
AUDNZD, with macro factors pointing toward a move closer to 1.13. NOKSEK also screens as carrying a meaningful risk  
premium versus these factors, pointing to fair value closer to 0.94.
